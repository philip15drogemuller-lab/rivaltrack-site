Privacy Policy: RivalTrack

Last updated: 9 November 2026

RivalTrack is designed to keep your data on your phone. This policy explains what is stored where, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Workouts, routines, exercises, sets and personal records
  • Your food and calorie log, nutrition goals and body stats (age, height, weight, activity level)
  • Steps and active energy read from Apple Health, and only if you switch them on: sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, body weight and VO₂ max. They are only shown in the app, never uploaded and never used for advertising
  • Photos of meals. These are recognized on the phone with Apple's built-in image recognition
  • Your weigh-ins, and what the app learns about how you measure foods (drink or food, usual portion)
  • Water you log, your health badges, step streaks and reminder settings
  • Your usual gym and how often you pick each gym (to suggest gyms), stored only on the phone
  • Readiness scores, Watch Report ratings and nutrition evaluations: worked out on the phone from your own data, never uploaded
  • GPS routes are also saved to Apple Health if you allow it, so they appear in the Fitness app
  • GPS routes and location. If you track an outdoor session with GPS, your location is used to measure distance, pace, elevation and km splits. The route is saved only on your phone and is never uploaded or shared. Location is only used while a GPS session is running (including with the phone locked), never in the background otherwise

Data shared online (only if you use the Friends feature)

When you agree to use Friends, the following is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, EU region), using an anonymous sign-in with no email, phone number or password:

  • Your chosen display name and your invite code
  • The date you first started using the app (to recognise early users as Founding Members)
  • Workouts you mark as All friends or Selected friends: title, date, duration, exercise names, sets, weights, reps and personal records
  • For GPS sessions: the distance and that it was measured by GPS (never the route or your location)
  • Photo proof you choose to attach to a cardio session (a photo of a machine's display), visible only to the people who can see that session
  • While you train, if your workouts are shared with friends or you join a friend's session: a "training now" status with the workout title, live distance or number of sets, deleted when you finish
  • Challenges you create or join (including team assignments and face-off lifts you log)
  • Which badges you've earned (badge names only), so friends can see them
  • Anonymous food knowledge (can be turned off in Settings → Help improve food recognition): when you log a food, a vote on whether the product is a drink or food and the amount, counted per barcode and per word of the product name, without your ID; and products you type in for barcodes Open Food Facts doesn't have (stored with your anonymous user ID so only you can edit them). Nothing about what or when you eat is linked to you
  • Planned training sessions you create or are invited to: date and time, the gym's name, address and map position, an optional note, who is invited and who is coming. Visible only to the people invited
  • In a face-off duel: your body weight and sex (for the tiebreak and DOTS points), visible only to your opponent
  • Your friend connections, people you have blocked, and 💪 likes
  • Reports you send about other users

Shared workouts are only visible to the friends you have connected with (or the friends you selected). Private workouts, food and calorie data and Apple Health data are never uploaded.

Legal basis: your explicit consent, given in the app before anything is uploaded (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your friends account.

Other services

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org): barcodes and search terms are sent to look up products. No personal data is included.
  • Apple Health: workouts and meals are written to Apple Health only if you allow it. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Apple Maps: when you choose a gym, a search for fitness centres near your current location is sent to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Anonymous usage counts (Google Firebase, can be switched off in Settings): daily totals such as "a workout was finished", added up across all users. No user ID, device ID or personal data is sent.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you choose it to keep your friends account on a new phone, Apple confirms who you are; your email is not shared with RivalTrack.
  • Anthropic (Pro AI photo estimates only): when you tap "Estimate Every Item with AI", that meal photo and any details you type are sent through our server (Firebase, EU) to Anthropic's API for analysis. The photo isn't stored. Nothing is sent unless you use this feature.

Your rights and choices

  • Make any workout private when you finish it, or later in History.
  • Delete your friends account and everything you've shared: Friends → My Friends → Delete My Friends Account.
  • Export your data (JSON backup or CSV), or delete it from the phone: Settings → Backup & data.
  • Remove, block or report other users: Friends → My Friends.
  • Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to complain to your data protection authority (in Denmark: Datatilsynet).

Community rules

Offensive names, posts or content are not allowed. You can report or block any user. Reports are reviewed and content that breaks the rules is removed, usually within 24 hours.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing of data with third parties for marketing.

Health disclaimer

Calorie and macro targets are estimates for healthy adults and not medical advice. If you are pregnant, under 18, or have a medical condition or a history of eating disorders, consult a doctor or dietitian.

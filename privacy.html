Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what data the RivalTrack app uses, where it goes, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Most of what you log never leaves your phone.

Who is responsible

Drøgemüller Apps, CVR 46916034, Denmark, is responsible for the data described here (the “data controller”). Contact: philipdrogemuller@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

This is stored only on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud or Apple Health where noted). We never receive it.

  • Workouts, routines, exercises, sets and personal records
  • Your food and calorie log, nutrition goals and body stats (age, height, weight, activity level), weigh-ins and water
  • Data read from Apple Health, only if you allow it: steps and active energy, and if you switch them on, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, body weight, VO₂ max and the other readings under Sleep & Health
  • Readiness scores, Watch Report ratings and nutrition evaluations, worked out on the phone
  • Meal photos you recognise with the free on-phone recognition (Apple’s built-in image recognition)
  • GPS routes and your location during an outdoor session. The route is saved on your phone, and in Apple Health if you allow it. Your location is otherwise only used when you look for a gym near you: that search goes to Apple Maps
  • Your usual gyms, reminders, badges, settings and what the app learns about how you measure foods
  • The date you first opened the app, kept in the phone’s Keychain (and in your iCloud key-value storage if available), so the free Pro trial can be used once per person

Data we process online

When the app opens

When the app opens, it reads one setting from our server at Google Firebase: whether RivalTrack Pro is switched on. No name or other personal details are sent with it. Like any internet request it reaches Google with your device’s IP address, it can include an App Check token (see App Check), and if you use the friends feature or AI estimates it carries your anonymous account’s sign-in.

Apart from that, nothing below is sent unless you use the feature it belongs to.

Friends (optional)

The friends feature only starts after you agree to it on the Friends screen. It uses an anonymous account: no email, phone number or password. If you choose “Keep your account” (Sign in with Apple), Apple gives us an identifier for your Apple ID so you can sign in again on a new phone; Apple doesn’t share your email with us. If you agree, the following is stored on our servers at Google Firebase:

  • Your display name, your invite code and the date you first started using the app
  • Workouts you share with all friends or selected friends: title, date, duration, exercise names, sets, weights, reps, personal records and badges earned in that workout. For GPS sessions only the distance and that it was measured by GPS, never the route or your location
  • Your best lifts on bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press and pull-ups, worked out from workouts you share with all friends, for the rankings
  • Photo proof you choose to attach to a cardio session
  • While you train with sharing on, or join a friend’s session: a “training now” status with the workout title and live distance or number of sets, deleted when you finish
  • Challenges and face-offs you create or join, including the lifts logged and, in a face-off, your body weight and sex (used for the tiebreak and DOTS points and shown to your opponent)
  • Planned training sessions you create or are invited to: date, time, the gym’s name, address and map position, an optional note and who is coming
  • Your friend connections and requests, people you block, 💪 likes, and who invited you (to credit invite rewards)
  • Reports you send about other users
  • If you allow notifications for the friends feature: your device’s push token and app language, so friends’ activity can be sent to you
  • If “Help improve food recognition” is on (it can be switched off in Settings): when you log a food, an anonymous vote on whether the product is a drink or a food and the usual amount, counted per barcode and per word of the product name without your ID; and products you type in for unknown barcodes, stored with your anonymous ID so only you can edit them

Shared workouts are only shown to the friends you connected with, or the friends you selected. Your display name and best lifts can be looked up by other signed-in users who know your account ID, for example friends and people you have sent requests to. Private workouts, your food log and Apple Health data are never uploaded.

Why we may use it: your consent, given on the Friends screen. Because workouts can say something about your health, this is your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your friends account (see Deleting your data).

AI meal photo estimates (Pro, optional)

When you use the AI estimate, the meal photo and any details you type are sent to our server (Google Cloud Functions in Google’s EU region europe-west1), which passes them to Anthropic’s Claude API to estimate the food and calories. Our server does not save the photo. Anthropic processes it under its Commercial Terms, which say Anthropic may not train its models on this content, and its Data Processing Addendum. Anthropic may process the data outside the EU.

To limit each person to a number of estimates a day, the feature uses an anonymous account and we keep a count of how many estimates that account has used today, plus a total for all users. The app asks for your permission before the first photo is sent.

Why we may use it: your consent, given when you first use the feature.

Partner codes (optional)

If you enter a code from a creator or coach, we store that your anonymous account joined with that code and when. If you later buy Pro, a random token is attached to the purchase so our server can credit that partner. Apple then tells our server about the purchase and any refund. We record the product, price, currency, date, App Store country and a transaction number; never your name, email or payment details. Partners only see totals, never who bought.

Why we may use it: to pay partners correctly for the purchases they referred, which you choose to support by entering their code, and to keep the records bookkeeping law requires.

Usage counts (off unless you switch them on)

If you switch on “Share anonymous usage counts” (during setup or in Settings), the app counts events such as “a workout was finished” or “the Pro screen was opened” on your phone and sends daily totals at most once an hour. The totals are added up across all users and stored without any user or device ID. Switch it off at any time in Settings.

Why we may use it: your consent, given when you switch it on.

App Check

When the app talks to our server, it can include a short-lived token from Google Firebase App Check, based on Apple’s App Attest, that shows the request comes from the genuine app. This protects the service from abuse. According to Google, App Check tokens last at most 7 days.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app which purchases you have so Pro can be unlocked. Except for partner codes (above), we do not store your purchases on our servers.

How long we keep data

  • Friends data: until you delete it or your friends account, except what is listed under Deleting your data
  • “Training now” status: until the session ends
  • AI estimate counts: one counter per anonymous account, holding only the date and the count, which starts again from zero on a new day; the anonymous account itself stays until deleted
  • Partner code and purchase records: as long as needed to pay the partner and for bookkeeping
  • Usage counts: daily totals with no personal data
  • Google keeps logged IP addresses from sign-in for a few weeks, according to Google

Deleting your data

In the app: Friends → My Friends → Delete My Friends Account. This deletes your profile and best lifts, your shared workouts and photo proof, your friend connections and requests, your block list, your push token, your invite code, sessions you planned, challenges you created and your “training now” status, and then deletes the anonymous account itself. Firebase only allows that last step if you signed in recently; if it fails, the account is left without any of the data above.

Some things are not removed by that button at the moment:

  • 💪 likes you gave on friends’ workouts, and your entries in challenges and planned sessions that others created
  • Products you typed in for unknown barcodes, and reports you sent
  • The record of who invited you, and partner code and purchase records
  • The anonymous account and daily count used for AI estimates, if you used them without the friends feature

If you want these removed too, email philipdrogemuller@gmail.com before deleting your friends account and include the invite code shown in the app, so we can find them. An anonymous account used only for AI estimates has no invite code or name, so we can’t find it from an email; it holds nothing but the daily count. None of these records contain your email, phone number or real name unless you used it as your display name.

Data on your phone: Settings → Backup & data lets you export it (JSON or CSV) or delete it. Deleting the app also deletes it, except what is in Apple Health or iCloud.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to get it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Email philipdrogemuller@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Children

The friends feature and the AI estimates are for people aged 15 or over. If you are under 15, a parent or guardian must agree before you use them. Everything else in the app works without an account.

Health information

Calorie and macro targets, readiness scores and health ratings are estimates for general fitness, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, under 18, or have a medical condition or a history of eating disorders, talk to a doctor or dietitian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change affects what is shared, the app will ask again before sharing more.

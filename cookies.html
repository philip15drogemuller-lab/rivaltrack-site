Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads, tracking, external fonts, scripts or embedded content from other sites. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as any web server does.

The app

The app has no cookies, ads or tracking. Like any app, it stores data on your phone so its features work: your workouts, food log and settings, sign-in details for the features you choose to use, and the date you first opened the app (so the free trial can be used once per person).

The only optional data is anonymous usage counts. They are off unless you switch them on during setup or in Settings. When they are on, the app keeps a count of events on your phone until it sends the daily totals. See the Privacy Policy.

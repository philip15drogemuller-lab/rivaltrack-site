Health and safety

RivalTrack is a fitness and food logging app, not a medical device and not medical advice. Calorie and macro targets, readiness scores, health ratings, warm-ups and suggestions are estimates. AI meal estimates can be wrong: check them before you rely on them. Train within your limits, use a spotter for heavy lifts, and talk to a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of eating disorders.

Refunds and Cancellations

Last updated: 11 November 2026

RivalTrack is free to download. If RivalTrack Pro is offered in your country, it is sold through Apple’s App Store, so Apple takes the payment and handles cancellations and refunds. We never receive your payment details and can’t issue refunds ourselves.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancel in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple’s help: If you want to cancel a subscription from Apple.

Free trial

A free trial, when offered, is shown in the app before you buy. If it turns into a paid subscription, that is shown before you confirm, and you can cancel before it ends.

Asking for a refund

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com: sign in, choose “I’d like to”, then “Request a refund”, and pick RivalTrack. Apple decides on refunds under its terms and the consumer law where you live. Your statutory rights are not affected.

If something doesn’t work

If Pro doesn’t unlock or a feature you paid for doesn’t work, email philipdrogemuller@gmail.com. We will try to fix it, and can point you to the right place at Apple.
